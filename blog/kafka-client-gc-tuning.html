Cutting client startup from 2.6 min to 40 seconds with GC tuning

JVM · based on work at Flipkart

A Kafka client library took 2.6 minutes to start. The instinct was to blame the network; the profiler said otherwise.

What the profiler showed

Java Flight Recorder revealed long young-GC pauses during initialization: the client allocated heavily at startup, and default heap/GC settings turned that into stop-the-world churn.

The fix

Right-sizing the young generation, picking a lower-pause collector for the allocation pattern, and trimming throwaway allocations at startup brought initialization down to 40 seconds — no architectural rewrite required.

Takeaway

Profile before scaling. A 4× startup win came from GC ergonomics, not more hardware.
