Closing the loop: ads performance goals as feedback

Ads platforms · based on work at Flipkart

Most ads tooling reports metrics. Goal optimisation goes one step further: it feeds performance metrics back into the system so campaigns steer toward their targets automatically.

The design

Small, single-purpose microservices each own one goal type. They read aggregated delivery and performance signals, compute the gap to target, and emit pacing or bidding adjustments through the same campaign-management APIs advertisers use — no shadow paths.

Why it worked

Treating goals as feedback control rather than dashboards changed the question from "what happened?" to "how far off target are we, and what corrects it?" Roughly 45% of advertising teams went on to achieve over 100% of their targets after adoption.

Lesson

Keep the loop small and observable: one metric in, one adjustment out, with a design doc (HLD/LLD) that states the control law explicitly. Distributed systems discipline (Storm topologies, Kafka, HBase underneath) matters, but the feedback framing is what made it legible.
